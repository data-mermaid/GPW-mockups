Data Sources

The population data used in this platform is derived from the following authoritative sources:

  • Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL) — European Commission Joint Research Centre
  • WorldPop — University of Southampton
  • LandScan — Oak Ridge National Laboratory
  • GPWv4 — NASA Socioeconomic Data and Applications Center (SEDAC)
  • National census data from participating governments, harmonised to 2000–2025

Spatial Resolution

All raster products are provided at 30 arc-second (~1 km) spatial resolution at the equator. Watershed summaries are computed at the HydroSHEDS level-4 basin delineation. Administrative boundaries follow the GADM 4.1 dataset.

Temporal Coverage

  • 2000 — baseline census year
  • 2005 — interpolated estimate
  • 2015 — interpolated estimate
  • 2020 — most recent census anchor
  • 2025 — modelled projection

Methodology

Population counts are disaggregated from administrative units to grid cells using a dasymetric mapping approach. Land cover data from ESA CCI Land Cover is used as ancillary information to restrict settlement areas. Uninhabited pixels (open water, bare rock, permanent ice) are masked prior to redistribution.

Uncertainty Estimation

Pixel-level uncertainty is quantified using a Monte Carlo framework with 1 000 iterations. The inter-quartile range of the resulting distribution is reported as the uncertainty band for each grid cell and aggregated region. For further detail see:

  • Stevens et al. (2015) — Disaggregating census data for population mapping using multiple imputation
  • Wardrop et al. (2018) — Spatially disaggregated population estimates in the absence of national population and housing census data
  • Leyk et al. (2019) — The spatial allocation of population: a review of large-scale gridded population data products and their fitness for use

Coral Reef Regions

Reef boundaries are derived from the UNEP-WCMC Global Coral Reef Monitoring Network dataset, version 4.1. The five biogeographic regions follow the classification of Spalding et al. (2007) — Marine Ecoregions of the World. Watershed intersections are computed using the HydroSHEDS river basin layer at Pfafstetter level 4.

Validation

Modelled population surfaces are validated against independent household survey data from the DHS Program and national population registers where available. Root-mean-square error (RMSE) and mean absolute error (MAE) are reported per region and time step in the supplementary materials.
